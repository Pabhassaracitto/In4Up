# Prompt 6 Detail — Home Command Center

> Gửi prompt này vào Google Stitch sau khi đã tạo Home Prompt 6. Tạo từng state riêng, không chỉ một Desktop và một Mobile tổng quan.

> Keep Home as a calm command center, not a dashboard. Preserve the five-workspace shell, one dominant Continue Learning action, light Needs Attention, limited Next Actions, progressive disclosure, and Quiet Lexicon. Do not add leaderboard, streak pressure, or every tool as a card.
>
> Create these states separately:
>
> 1. Mobile — First launch/no history with a clear first action.
> 2. Desktop — First launch/no history with restrained onboarding and no fake activity.
> 3. Mobile — Populated Home with Continue card, Next Actions, Needs Attention, and Recent Activity.
> 4. Desktop — Populated Home with restrained two-column layout.
> 5. Mobile — Continue card populated with one primary action and one secondary action.
> 6. Desktop — Continue card populated with resume reading/listening context.
> 7. Continue card stale state when source changed, deleted, or unavailable; show recovery action.
> 8. Needs Attention empty state.
> 9. Needs Attention populated state with due review and unfinished note, without alarm-heavy styling.
> 10. Quick Capture opened, typing, saved, cancelled, and draft-restored states.
> 11. Loading/offline Home with cached content and clear sync state.
> 12. Recent Activity expanded and collapsed state.
> 13. Desktop responsive transition from two columns to constrained layout.
> 14. Mobile one-column scroll with Mini Player and bottom navigation safe-area.
>
> For every state describe visible/hidden elements, primary action, stale/offline behavior, focus/keyboard behavior, scroll position, and whether the user can recover the previous activity.
>
> Constraints:
>
> - One Continue action is visually dominant.
> - Next Actions maximum 3–4 items.
> - Needs Attention is useful, not alarming.
> - Do not hard-code shortcut `N` for Quick Capture without a shortcut registry.
> - Do not turn Recent Activity or Focus Rhythm into a statistics dashboard.
